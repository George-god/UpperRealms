# Cultivation and Cosmology

**Status:** Existing story language and design direction. Metaphysical mechanisms remain open.

## The layers of knowledge

1. **Observed:** Qi is tangible to cultivators and manifests in the environment. It can circulate through meridians and be cultivated.
2. **Taught:** Manuals, elders and sect traditions describe how to refine or direct that energy, though they may disagree.
3. **Unknown:** Why the Dao exists, whether fate is fixed, what the cracked heavens signify and what the Outer Gods truly are.

Narrative claims should indicate which layer they occupy.

## Qi and the first cultivation moment — Established scene

Spirit Veil Forest is saturated with luminous energy, but Lin Mei describes its qi as “sick.” A corrupted beast appears nearby. After surviving the fight, the protagonist experiences energy gathering within their body and is identified in the prologue as entering **Qi Condensation**. Lin Mei observes that they did so *without a manual* and is shocked.

The story establishes an *extraordinary occurrence*, not its cause. Do not attribute it to a particular bloodline, reincarnation, hidden immortal inheritance, or special class yet.

**Elder Shen's teaching:** “Cultivation is not power. It is agreement—between breath, heaven, and self.” Treat this as his philosophy until the worldbuilding defines whether this is literally how cultivation works.

## The Dao and tribulation

**Established creative direction:** The Heavenly Dao is central to the setting and ultimately worth protecting. The protagonist hears the dream declaration “The Dao is dying.” The speaker and reliability of this warning are unconfirmed.

**In-game account:** The public Codex calls certain ordeals the **Heavenly Tribunal** and characterizes tribulation as a response to ambition. This may be poetic or theological language.

**Open:** Does the Dao have will? Can it intervene? Can sects measure its condition? How do individual paths and universal laws interact? Avoid answering without an approved cosmology.

## Realms: avoid a silent naming conflict

The prologue explicitly names the first cultivation step **Qi Condensation**. Elsewhere the repository includes legacy realm assets named **Qi Refining**, **Foundation Building**, **Core Formation**, **Nascent Soul** and **Soul Transformation**. The exact authoritative progression names, stage count and gameplay requirements should be verified against live realm data and approved before a unified lore list is published.

Do **not** change realm IDs, migrations, numeric thresholds or the gameplay realm table on the strength of these notes. A possible naming reconciliation is to treat “Qi Refining” as a broad discipline and “Qi Condensation” as a first stage; this is **proposed**, not decided.

## Bloodlines, artifacts and manuals

- **Bloodlines:** Ancestral inheritances and rare awakenings are part of the broader design direction. Their origin and legendary bloodlines are not yet fixed as story canon.
- **Artifacts:** Relics may be equipped, used or awakened/evolved in gameplay design. Their creators and historical provenance require individual approval.
- **Manuals:** Cultivation instruction can be learned, traded or found. The protagonist's exceptional first awakening should **not** make all later learning meaningless.
- **Dao paths:** Differing approaches to cultivation provide identity and philosophical conflict. No path is canonically “correct” by default.

## Plane and scale boundaries

The name **UpperRealms** does not yet fix the number, names or access rules for upper planes. Major ascension details have been intentionally deferred. The world should feel vast without mapping every heaven before the first story arc needs it.

## Writer guardrails

- Never claim every powerful character is aware of the Dao's crisis.
- Avoid declaring that all corruption comes from one cosmic antagonist.
- Keep cultivation and moral choices meaningfully plural.
- Do not have the protagonist effortlessly surpass established experts just because of the opening anomaly.
- Any future metaphysical reveal must reconcile Lin Mei's observations, Elder Shen's teaching and the dream without treating every observation as a perfect explanation.
