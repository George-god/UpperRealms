# World History and the Unreliable Record

**Status:** Established source texts and open historical chronology; no fixed calendar yet.

## What the world says about its origins

**In-game account — “Before the First Breath”:** The existing History & Lore Codex attributes the emergence of the Upper Realms to primordial qi separating into heaven, earth and the space between. This is what **archivists teach**, not yet a creator-confirmed account of creation. See [Codex catalog](../../app/Services/Codex/CodexCatalogSyncService.php).

**In-game account — “The Heavenly Tribunal”:** Another Codex chronicle depicts tribulations as heaven's judgement, speaking of fire, void and heart-demons. Whether a literal Tribunal exists, or whether this is religious metaphor for the laws of cultivation, remains **open**.

**In-game account — “Cycles of Eras”:** Legends survive changing eras, while sect standings and rankings turn over. The text ties into game seasons; do **not** equate a mechanical season reset with a literal cosmic apocalypse without approval.

**In-game account — “Fallen Sects”:** Remnants of ruined sects hold dangerous manuals and secrets. The exact sect names and dates are unestablished.

## Candidate epoch framework — **Proposed, not canon**

One option for future author review is a sequence of eras such as **Age of First Breath → Age of Ascendant Sects → Era of Lost Records → Current Age**. These are *working labels only*. No timeline, dates, ancient war, Dao fracture or destroyed civilization has yet been approved as historical fact.

A “Dao Fracture” was previously floated during brainstorming. The prologue dream's cracked sky **does not by itself establish** that such a historical event occurred.

## Historical knowledge by audience

- **Village residents:** Local rumors about Spirit Veil, dangers of corrupted qi, respect for sects and their power.
- **Cultivators and sect archivists:** More technical cultivation traditions, histories of sect rise and collapse, records that may disagree.
- **Readers of forbidden chronicles:** Potentially older sources whose accuracy should itself be a plot point.
- **Player at prologue start:** No reliable personal memory; limited immediate observation only.
