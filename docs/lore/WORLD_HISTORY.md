# World History and the Unreliable Record

**Status:** Canon Checkpoint 01: approved ancient sequence; existing in-world accounts retained; no fixed calendar.

## What the world says about its origins

**In-game account — “Before the First Breath”:** The existing History & Lore Codex attributes the emergence of the Upper Realms to primordial qi separating into heaven, earth and the space between. This is what **archivists teach**, not yet a creator-confirmed account of creation. See [Codex catalog](../../app/Services/Codex/CodexCatalogSyncService.php).

**In-game account — “The Heavenly Tribunal”:** Another Codex chronicle depicts tribulations as heaven's judgement, speaking of fire, void and heart-demons. Whether a literal Tribunal exists, or whether this is religious metaphor for the laws of cultivation, remains **open**.

**In-game account — “Cycles of Eras”:** Legends survive changing eras, while sect standings and rankings turn over. The text ties into game seasons; do **not** equate a mechanical season reset with a literal cosmic apocalypse without approval.

**In-game account — “Fallen Sects”:** Remnants of ruined sects hold dangerous manuals and secrets. The exact sect names and dates are unestablished.

## Candidate epoch framework — **Proposed, not canon**

One option for future author review is a sequence of eras such as **Age of First Breath → Age of Ascendant Sects → Era of Lost Records → Current Age**. These are *working labels only*. These era names and exact dates remain unapproved. The ancient sequence below supersedes the earlier absence of approved ancient history.

A “Dao Fracture” was previously floated during brainstorming. The prologue dream's cracked sky **does not by itself establish** that such a historical event occurred.

## Historical knowledge by audience

- **Village residents:** Local rumors about Spirit Veil, dangers of corrupted qi, respect for sects and their power.
- **Cultivators and sect archivists:** More technical cultivation traditions, histories of sect rise and collapse, records that may disagree.
- **Readers of forbidden chronicles:** Potentially older sources whose accuracy should itself be a plot point.
- **Player at prologue start:** No reliable personal memory; limited immediate observation only.

## Checkpoint 01 — established ancient history (developer truth)

1. Beyond the Sanctuary lies the greater reality provisionally called the Boundless Expanse, containing other universes and beings. The Eternal Concord (working name) carried out the Great Returning, dismantling dying universes for primordial resources. The First Divine Being was a survivor and defector, rejecting the destruction of a universe and escaping with survivors and the possibility of creating a refuge. The Concord's full motives, the exact existential danger and details of the defection remain open; Architect of Endings and Seed of Creation are working concepts, not fixed details.
2. The First Being created the Sanctuary as a protected experimental universe in which life could continue without the Great Returning. Isolation and protection coexist uneasily with the need for evolution. The creator did not foresee all consequences. The Heavenly Dao emerged from relationships between its laws, distinct from the creator.
3. During the Ancient War, outsider forces discovered and threatened the Sanctuary. The First Being and First Ancients defended it; the Dao responded through reality, not speech. Outsiders are not a single inherently evil species or faction. The term Outer Gods reflects an in-world category.
4. The Last Covenant and Sundering divided the First Being into two genuine aspects, not an original and a copy: Eternal Vanguard (working name), operating beyond the Sanctuary to defend it, and Eternal Foundation (working name), distributed among the Primordial Anchors sustaining the Sanctuary. Their distinct experiences and memories developed different priorities. Neither is a villain or the Dao.
5. The Changing Anchors: the Foundation attempted to adapt an Anchor whose rigidity resisted potentially healthy Resonance; the Vanguard reinforced its original configuration, fearing attack during transition. The incompatible protections produced the First Fracture, and outside forces exploited the vulnerability. Neither aspect had complete information. The region became the Scarred Realm, containing damage and healthy transformed ecosystems. A briefly stable transformed configuration is evidence for research, not proof of a universal repair method.
6. The Nine Terraces, a scholarly civilization already present in the region, fell during the catastrophe. In the Great Scattering, Exiles escaped to wider Sanctuary, Hidden Hearths survived in small pockets, and the Lost remained unaccounted for. The Unfinished Record tradition preserved observations and explicitly unresolved conclusions. The Ninth Terrace (Terrace of Unfinished Questions) sealed dangerous unfinished research. Han Zhiren, an evacuation engineer, led survivors through the Lantern Causeway toward the refuge that became Lantern Hollow.
7. Over long ages the Scarred Realm evolved. The Seal of Ink and Root adapted beyond its custodians' expectations, and scattered traditions spread through families, students, migrations and local exchange. Lantern Hollow persisted through generations, with changes and rebuilding possible; no hidden empire or exclusive divine bloodline is implied.
8. Present cultivation age: the player awakens in Spirit Veil Forest. Most people know only fragments or legends about ancient history.

No fixed dates, ages, era lengths or cosmological scale beyond these relationships are established. The nine scholarly centers' working disciplines include astronomy, archives, ecology, Qi currents, formation architecture, resonance, navigation/evacuation, protection, and anomalous interdisciplinary study. The exact institutional names other than the Ninth's working designation remain flexible. Historical chronology is distinct from the order in which players discover it.
