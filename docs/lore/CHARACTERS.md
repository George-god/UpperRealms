# Characters and Relationship Register

**Status:** Canon Checkpoint 01: established opening and approved character foundations; future actions labelled separately.

## The Protagonist — Established point of view

**Entry:** Awakens on cold ground in Spirit Veil Forest without recollection of their name, past or arrival. The first memory-like impressions are thunder, fire and a cracking sky.

**Observed anomaly:** Survives an environment Lin Mei considers lethal to mortals and condenses qi without a manual.

**Player agency:** Do not canonize a default name, gender, moral nature, prior allegiance, bloodline, reincarnation, immortal identity or ultimate fate. The protagonist's history must not rob players of meaningful present-day choices.

**Knowledge at scene end:** They have met a human ally, seen corrupted qi, experienced the first cultivation and heard a troubling dream voice. They have not learned what the voice means.

## Lin Mei — Established companion/guide

**Role:** Herbalist Disciple; enters the forest carrying an herb basket, initially raises it defensively when surprised by the protagonist. Cautious, direct and willing to help once survival requires cooperation.

**What she knows:** The Spirit Veil is dangerous, its qi appears sick, cultivators avoid its depths, the village ward needs clean herbs and spontaneous qi condensation is highly unusual.

**What she does not necessarily know:** The protagonist's origin, the true cause of corruption or the nature of the Dao's crisis.

**Canon character direction:** Lin Mei values her village and seeks to become capable of protecting it; her long-term vocation is traveling healer and researcher. Her choice to help the stranger exposes her to sect attention. Give her competencies and decisions after the tutorial; avoid reducing her to a quest icon or guaranteed romantic interest.

**Dialogue tone:** Practical and sometimes startled; empathy follows observation and action, not blind trust.

## Elder Shen — Established village elder

**Role:** Senior Riverstone figure with enough cultivation knowledge to teach patience and foundations.

**Worldview:** Cultivation is harmony or agreement, not simply the accumulation of force. He advises meditation, proper advancement and caution about attracting sect scrutiny.

**Open:** His age, cultivation realm, former affiliations and how much he suspects about the forest. Do not reveal a hidden legendary identity without approval.

**Dialogue tone:** Reflective, precise and economical. Warns without lengthy exposition.

## Messenger of the Azure Gate — Established function

**Role:** Unnamed outer-sect emissary arriving at dawn, seeking the Spirit Veil survivor.

**Knowledge:** Detects that something is unusual about the protagonist's meridians; says they are “as if heaven wrote you twice.”

**Open:** Name, exact sect, motives, loyalty and whether this observation is metaphor, diagnosis or something more dangerous. Their uncertainty is a feature of the scene.

**Dialogue tone:** Controlled, observant, authoritative without omniscience.

## Supporting people and threats

- **Riverstone villagers:** Understand local risks; fear is justified and can soften through player actions.
- **Corrupted Spirit Wolf:** First dangerous enemy. Its corruption is visible; its origin is **not** established.
- **Unknown dream voice:** Heard saying “The Dao is dying.” Do not ascribe identity, truthfulness or moral alignment.
- **Outer Gods:** In-world category for outsiders, including historical attackers; not all outsiders are hostile, and no individual is introduced or identified in the opening.

## Planned future relationships and destinations

Lin Mei learns from Hua's writings after the unchanged tutorial, then meets her through regional research. Informal mentorship grows into intellectual partnership; formal discipleship remains open. Lin Mei retains local ties and independent judgment. Her eventual Divine Healer manifestation and personal Grace of Living Harmony are planned future, after accumulated achievements, not automatic consequences of meeting the player. Hua's own future institutional role remains flexible.

Yun and Lin Mei become collaborators, eventually peers with distinct expertise. Yun's possible renewed Grace of the Quiet Interval is planned future, not currently active. Pei becomes a collaborator through critical testing, not a villain. Other characters' future roles in the Open Lantern, the Pale Thread crisis and Scarred Realm expedition are outlined in MAIN_STORY.md. Do not assume a character knows developer-only history, or bind an NPC's entire future to one player's actions.
