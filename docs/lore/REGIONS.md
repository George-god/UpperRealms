# Regions and Environmental Storytelling

**Status:** Two established opening locations; broader geography deliberately open.

## Spirit Veil Forest — Established

**Atmosphere:** Dense mist, ancient roots, floating spirit-light, unnaturally quiet air, perceived spiritual pressure. The game's description calls it *“Where mist remembers what mortals forget.”*

**Immediate risks:** Distorted or “sick” qi, hostile spirit creatures and isolation. Lin Mei says ordinary mortals cannot endure the Veil and cultivators avoid going deep. This is **her account**, not an externally verified numerical rule.

**Physical story anchors in implemented prologue:**
- A **shattered jade plaque** with partially erased characters; a surviving line reads “…beyond the veil, none return.”
- A **moss-covered spirit stone**, long-abandoned offerings and fresh claw marks.
- A **Corrupted Spirit Wolf** with violet eyes and a hostile presence.

**Narrative role:** Opening mystery and sensory tutorial. Its strangeness asks “Why did you survive?” rather than answering it. Do not make the entire forest an all-explaining exposition zone.

**Unanswered:** Location relative to major sects; whether the forest is a barrier, sanctuary, prison or wound; how long its qi has been corrupted.

## Riverstone Village — Established

**Atmosphere:** Paper lanterns, stone road, mountain wind, guarded windows and worried residents. The location tagline is *“Lanterns on the mountain road.”*

**Village life:** Spirit herb processing supports a protective ward. Residents may depend on cultivators but still fear power they cannot understand. Elder Shen helps provide the first interpretive framework for qi.

**Narrative role:** Safety after danger; first community with needs; a personal reason to care about the wider world's threats.

**What players learn here:** Not every problem is resolved by combat. Aid, patience and a credible guide can earn provisional trust.

**Unanswered:** Population, political allegiance, nearest city, economy, exact map coordinates and travel duration to the Spirit Veil.

## Route from forest to village

**Established:** Lin Mei accompanies or guides the protagonist out of the forest to Riverstone. The prologue moves from the first awakening to the village and eventually to morning after a dream. No measured geography, movement speed or fixed map topology has been defined.

**Proposal for later scene design:** A short descending trail where the spiritual pressure lessens and the first lantern lights are seen. It should be emotionally memorable without adding a long mandatory walking segment.

## Additional regions

The game has a world region system and Codex catalogue driven by game data. Those records do **not** automatically establish the story geography or travel relationships between locations. Any new named region should enter this file with:
- relationship to Riverstone or another fixed landmark;
- environmental identity and gameplay purpose;
- local peoples and institutions;
- what outsiders believe versus what is actually known;
- the chapter where it first appears.

**Rule:** Do not invent mountains, distances, neighboring cities or faction borders in implementation prompts until we approve them.
